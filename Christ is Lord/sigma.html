How I get to know about Christianity.

As a child, I never thought I would become a Christian. Growing up in a Buddhist family, I was always taught that Christianity was possessed and demonized. However, when I was 12, a Christian organization called FGACYC came to my school to host a camp called Wuga Wuga. I’ve always loved events like this, so I begged my mom to let me go.

was my first interaction with Christians. The people were so kind and patient, and I had never felt so peaceful spending time with others. I started joining their activities and even participated in a cooking event twice. At the time, I didn’t fully understand what they did—I just went there to play and enjoy myself.

As time passed, I grew older and gradually forgot about the organization. But when I was 15, one of the organizers contacted my mom and asked if she would allow me to join an upcoming talent show.

How did I accept God.

During the show, I got to know a friend named Wei En. After the show, she contacted me and asked if I wanted to go to church with her. I said yes and went along. At first, I only went because there were lots of people my age there. I never closed my eyes while everyone prayed. I was swearing every single day, doing so many bad things throughout my life, and living miserably.

One day, a preacher asked me if I wanted to be a follower of Christ. I wanted to reject it but I felt like a voice was calling me, saying, "Accept it, Bernice, accept it." So, I walked up to him and prayed as he guided me. It was a prayer of me accepting Christ.

I didn’t fully understand what happened at that moment.During the prayer, my eyes was closed, but I saw an extremly strong light shinning towards me. Thinking back on it now, it must have been God.

How did accepting God changed my life completely

After accepting God, my cell group mates congratulated me. Everyone was so happy that a person had repented to God. Just one repentance made them all happy. I felt so loved in that moment, experiencing so many feelings I had never felt before.

As I slowly learned more about Christianity, I realized that this is not just a religion—it is a relationship with God. I came to understand how lovely my God is. He is strong, cool, amazing, and just perfect.

After reading the Bible, I realized how bad of a person I was in the past. So, I stopped swearing, stopped sinning, and started spreading the gospel to people around me. Seeing people receive the love of God through me truly warms my heart.

I guess I will never leave God, because without God, I am nothing.
